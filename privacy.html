Privacy Policy

This page explains what Savard Construction may receive when someone uses this website to ask about a project.

Information you choose to send

The quote form may ask for your name, phone number, email address, project type, project location, and a description of the work you are considering. You can also contact Savard Construction directly by phone or email.

How inquiries are used

Information sent to Savard Construction is used to understand your request, respond to you, discuss whether the work is a fit, and communicate about a possible quote or project. It is not used to create an account on this website.

Where information goes

The form is designed to send an inquiry through the website's hosting and email configuration. The exact mail or hosting provider depends on the production server. TODO: confirm the provider and delivery settings before launch.

Cookies and outside links

The site may use basic browser storage or cookies required by its interface and may load services used for fonts, animations, galleries, or form handling. No analytics service is described here as active unless it is later configured. Links to email, phone, social platforms, or other websites are governed by those destinations' own practices.

Protecting submitted details

Savard Construction will use reasonable care when handling information received through the site. No website or email route can be described as completely risk-free, so please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other information that is not needed to discuss your project.
