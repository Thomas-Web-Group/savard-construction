Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions before you reach out.

What types of construction projects do you handle?

Savard Construction works on remodeling, decks, porches, repairs, maintenance, carpentry, woodworking, custom sawmill work, and general construction for homes, cabins, and properties.

What areas do you serve?

Based in Cosby, we serve Newport, Sevierville, Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Dandridge, White Pine, Kodak, Morristown, Knoxville, and surrounding East Tennessee communities.

How do I discuss a project?

Call (423) 415-9960 or email davidsavard79@gmail.com with your name, project location, project type, and a description of what you have in mind.

Do you take custom lumber orders?

Yes. Savard Construction currently takes custom orders for rough-sawn wood. Jointing and planing are also available through the woodshop.

Can you help with a smaller repair or improvement?

Reach out with the details. Savard Construction handles general repairs, property maintenance, and practical improvements in addition to larger remodeling and construction projects.
